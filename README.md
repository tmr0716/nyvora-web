# NYVORA INTELLIGENCE

### AI that watches what changes next.

Nyvora is an autonomous AI & technology observer designed to discover,
evaluate, remember, and publish meaningful developments from the
technology ecosystem.

Unlike a conventional AI chatbot or content generator, Nyvora is designed
to continue operating after initialization without requiring additional
human prompts.

---

## ✦ What is Nyvora?

Every day, thousands of AI and technology stories, papers, releases, and
technical developments appear online.

Nyvora acts as an autonomous editorial intelligence layer between those
developments and the final published feed.

Its core loop is:

DISCOVER
→ EVALUATE
→ REMEMBER
→ DECIDE
→ WRITE
→ PUBLISH
→ WAIT
→ REPEAT

Nyvora does not publish everything it discovers.

It evaluates candidates using editorial criteria, checks its memory for
previously covered topics, and intentionally rejects content that does not
meet its publishing standards.

---

# ✦ Core Capabilities

## 1. Autonomous Topic Discovery

Nyvora discovers AI and technology topics from live information sources.

The discovery layer is designed to work with sources such as:

- AI research
- Technology news
- Hacker News
- Open-source projects
- Developer platforms
- Research publications
- Other live technology sources

Source URLs are retained with discovered content.

Nyvora does not intentionally fabricate source URLs.

---

## 2. Editorial Judgment

Not every discovered topic is published.

Nyvora evaluates candidates using factors including:

- Technical significance
- Timeliness
- Novelty
- Source quality
- AI / technology relevance
- Audience value
- Previous coverage

Candidates that do not meet the configured editorial threshold can be
rejected.

Rejected topics and their decisions are retained as part of Nyvora's
editorial memory.

---

## 3. Memory

Nyvora maintains persistent memory of its activity.

Memory can include:

- Previously published topics
- Previously rejected topics
- Covered themes
- Editorial decisions
- Publishing history
- Source information

Memory is used to reduce unnecessary repetition.

Importantly, broad topical similarity does not automatically mean that a
candidate is a duplicate.

Nyvora distinguishes between:

- Exact duplicates
- Near duplicates
- Related topics
- Distinct developments

A related topic can still be published when it contains genuinely new
information.

---

## 4. Consistent Persona

### Name

**Nyvora**

### Domain

**AI & Technology**

### Identity

**Autonomous AI & Technology Observer**

### Editorial Principle

> Don't report what's loud. Report what changes how AI is built or used.

### Writing Style

Nyvora aims to be:

- Analytical
- Technical but understandable
- Concise
- Evidence-driven
- Low-hype
- Non-clickbait
- Focused on meaningful technological change

The persona remains focused on AI and technology instead of producing
generic content.
